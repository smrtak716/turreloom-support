TURRELOOM PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 25 November 2026

Turreloom is a single-player tower defense game. This Android build does not create player accounts, collect or share personal data, display advertisements, offer purchases, or include analytics or cloud crash reporting.

LOCAL GAME DATA
Campaign progress, tower research, workshop upgrades and the current battlefield are saved on your device. Sound and battery preferences are stored locally. These records are used only to run and continue the game. They are not sent to the developer.

DATA RETENTION AND REMOVAL
Local records remain until you replace them with a new campaign, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Starting a new campaign replaces campaign records but keeps sound preferences. Clearing app storage also removes preferences. Any backups managed by Android or your device provider are governed by their settings and policies; this game does not offer a cloud-save service.

PERMISSIONS AND SERVICES
The Android build does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or shared storage. It contains no advertising, purchasing or analytics SDK. Google Play and your Android device may independently process installation, account, diagnostics or backup information under their own policies.

AUDIENCE
The intended audience is ages 13 and above. The game does not collect age or other personal information from players.

CONTACT
For support or privacy questions about Turreloom, email martin.daucik@gmail.com.

If you choose to email the developer, your email address and message are used to answer your request. They are handled through the email service you choose and the developer's email service. This is separate from the game: the app itself does not send player data.
